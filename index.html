03 / The research

Abstract

Multiplayer generative world models must depict a common scene through cameras that observe different parts of it. A shared spatial state constrains where entities appear, but leaves their visual identity ambiguous across views. We present a proxy-conditioned renderer that connects a shared 3D scene with joint two-player video generation.

Textureless map geometry, entity observations, and player cameras produce paired proxy videos; an RGB reference specifies the desired appearance. We concatenate the two views in a fixed layout and jointly denoise them through the native reference interface of a pretrained video transformer adapted with LoRA. Both views can thereby use appearance evidence from either camera while following their respective structural conditions.

We construct 7,846 paired gameplay examples and evaluate reference-conditioned appearance transfer on 68 cases with outputs from both our renderer and Seedance 2.5. Our renderer achieves higher DOVER (0.524 versus 0.469), LAION aesthetic scores, and edge alignment, together with higher automated cross-view player-identity scores (3.847/5 versus 3.552/5). Seedance retains stronger adjacent-frame DINO similarity and whole-view reference similarity, while qualitative examples illustrate cross-view identity and visibility differences.
